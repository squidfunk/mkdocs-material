# Change requests

To propose new features, see the [Zensical change-request guide].

  [Zensical change-request guide]: https://zensical.org/contributing/change-requests/

## How we manage change requests

Material for MkDocs accepts critical fixes and documentation corrections.
Existing requests and [archived discussions] remain available as a reference.

  [archived discussions]: https://github.com/squidfunk/mkdocs-material/discussions

## Report a problem

- __Critical bug:__ Follow our [bug reporting guide] and include a minimal
  reproduction.
- __Documentation error:__ Follow our [documentation issue guide] to report
  incorrect or missing information.
- __Security vulnerability:__ Follow our [security policy] to report it
  privately.

  [bug reporting guide]: reporting-a-bug.md
  [documentation issue guide]: reporting-a-docs-issue.md
  [security policy]: https://github.com/squidfunk/mkdocs-material/security/policy
